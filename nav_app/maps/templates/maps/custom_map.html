Instructions

  1. Upload a PDF or image map
  2. Define the coordinate range (min/max lat/lon)
  3. Choose the grid interval in minutes
  4. Click "Generate Graticule"
  5. Click on map to plot points
  6. Or enter coordinates to add points
💡 Tips:

• PDF files are automatically converted to images
• Coordinates are shown as you move the cursor
• Use minute intervals for nautical charts
• Enter coordinates in decimal or DMS format
